About the University

The University of Rwanda is a premier institution of higher learning dedicated to fostering academic excellence, groundbreaking research, and meaningful community engagement. With state-of-the-art facilities and a world-class faculty, we prepare our students to become leaders and innovators who will shape the future. Our commitment to quality education and sustainable development drives everything we do.

Mission: To provide quality, relevant and accessible higher education for sustainable development.

Vision: To be a centre of academic excellence and innovation for African development.
